# Report — THE WORLD IN ONE DAY, Sunday 22 November 2026

Six stories made the front page. Each was cross-verified from at least
two independent reports; the facts and sources used are listed below.

## 1. Lead — Benghazi, Libya

**Headline:** UKRAINE SINKS RUSSIAN CORVETTE AT LIBYAN PORT

Ukrainian sea drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury as it refuelled
at Benghazi naval base in eastern Libya. President Zelensky confirmed
Ukraine carried out the strike — the war's reach extends to North
Africa.

Sources: Open Chronicle; One Turn News; Daily Fox News.

## 2. Dymer, Ukraine

**Headline:** DRONE COLLAPSES DYMER APARTMENT BLOCK — AT LEAST 11 DEAD

A Russian Shahed drone destroyed a section of a five-storey apartment
building in Dymer, north of Kyiv, before dawn on Sunday. At least 11
residents were killed, and rescuers pulled a seven-year-old boy alive
from the rubble on Sunday evening.

Sources: One Turn News; Reuters (via DevDiscourse); Kyiv region
officials via EuropeSays/BytesEU.

## 3. Cikande, Banten, Indonesia

**Headline:** CANDY FACTORY FIRE: 24 BODY BAGS RECOVERED, DOZENS MISSING

Fire tore through the PT Fonusa Agung Mulia (Milkita) confectionery
plant in Cikande, Banten, from Saturday evening. On Sunday police
removed 24 body bags from the ruins, at least three deaths were
confirmed and dozens of workers remained unaccounted for, with DNA
identification under way.

Sources: AsiaToday (Reuters/detikNews); People (Associated Press);
Particle News.

## 4. Washington, United States

**Headline:** DEMOCRATS TAKE 258 HOUSE SEATS — LARGEST MAJORITY SINCE 1992

Democrat Chuck Hubbard's 330-vote recount victory over Republican
Virginia Foxx in North Carolina gave Democrats at least 258 House
seats, their largest majority since 1992. Hakeem Jeffries is set to
become Speaker.

Sources: Reuters (via LA Post); The Times; BBC (via iHeart/WVOC).

## 5. Bahrain / Strait of Hormuz

**Headline:** BAHRAIN SAYS IRAN HIT SITRA PLANT; TWO TANKERS STRUCK NEAR HORMUZ

Bahrain reported an Iranian attack on a civilian industrial facility
in Sitra that caused a fire and injuries, while two tankers were
struck in separate incidents near the Strait of Hormuz.

Sources: Open Chronicle; The Russell Record.

## 6. West Bank

**Headline:** SETTLER ATTACKS SCORCH WEST BANK VILLAGES

Israeli settlers attacked Palestinian communities across the occupied
West Bank, torching vehicles and daubing slogans. Israeli opposition
leader Gadi Eisenkot strongly condemned the attacks.

Sources: Open Chronicle; KPFA / Pacifica Evening News.
