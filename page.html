Lead Story · Benghazi, Libya

UKRAINE SINKS RUSSIAN CORVETTE AT LIBYAN PORT

Sea drones strike at Benghazi naval base · Zelensky confirms the attack

Ukrainian sea drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury as it refuelled at the Benghazi naval base in eastern Libya, in one of the most far-reaching strikes of the war.

President Zelensky confirmed that Ukraine carried out the strike. A Russian warship, hit while taking on fuel in a North African port, shows how far Kyiv’s uncrewed vessels can now reach — well beyond the Black Sea, into the Mediterranean world.

The sinking extends the war’s geography to North Africa and raises hard questions for every navy that believed a distant harbour was a safe one. Refuelling, once a routine pause, became the moment of greatest vulnerability for the Merkury and her crew.

Dymer, Ukraine

DRONE COLLAPSES DYMER APARTMENT BLOCK — AT LEAST 11 DEAD

A Russian Shahed drone destroyed a section of a five-storey apartment building in Dymer, north of Kyiv, before dawn on Sunday. At least 11 residents were killed. On Sunday evening rescuers pulled a seven-year-old boy alive from the rubble, as the search through the collapsed stairwell continued by floodlight.

Cikande, Banten, Indonesia

CANDY FACTORY FIRE: 24 BODY BAGS RECOVERED, DOZENS MISSING

Fire tore through the PT Fonusa Agung Mulia (Milkita) confectionery plant in Cikande, Banten, from Saturday evening. On Sunday police removed 24 body bags from the ruins. At least three deaths were confirmed and dozens of workers remained unaccounted for, with DNA identification under way for the victims.

Washington, United States

DEMOCRATS TAKE 258 HOUSE SEATS — LARGEST MAJORITY SINCE 1992

Democrat Chuck Hubbard’s 330-vote recount victory over Republican Virginia Foxx in North Carolina gave Democrats at least 258 House seats — their largest majority since 1992. Hakeem Jeffries is set to become Speaker when the new House convenes.

Bahrain / Strait of Hormuz

BAHRAIN SAYS IRAN HIT SITRA PLANT; TWO TANKERS STRUCK NEAR HORMUZ

Bahrain reported an Iranian attack on a civilian industrial facility in Sitra that caused a fire and injuries, while two tankers were struck in separate incidents near the Strait of Hormuz — the narrow waterway on which a large share of the world’s oil still depends.

West Bank

SETTLER ATTACKS SCORCH WEST BANK VILLAGES

Israeli settlers attacked Palestinian communities across the occupied West Bank, torching vehicles and daubing slogans. Israeli opposition leader Gadi Eisenkot strongly condemned the attacks, in a day of violence that left villages counting burned cars and broken calm.
